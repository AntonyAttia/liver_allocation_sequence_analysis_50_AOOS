**01_read_data.r** script imports four OPTN STAR datasets—the potential transplant recipient (PTR) file, the liver transplant file, the deceased donor file, and lab panels—sets a lower date bound of 9 December 2021 (FDA approval for liver NMP), and keeps only the variables needed for analysis.

**02_build_cohort.r** script builds the analysis cohort by identifying deceased-donor, single-organ liver transplants from 9 December 2021, linking each to its accepted offer, and excluding multi-organ, living-donor, split-liver, duplicate-donor, and directed-donation cases. It then classifies transplants into allocation pathway groups (<50, ≥50, allocation out-of-sequence), merges full recipient and donor details, and drops records missing essential information. Counts are produced for the cohort flow.

**03_calculate_variables.r** adds derived variables to the cohort: distinct centres contacted before the accepting centre, standardisation of key fields and units, donor risk index (DRI) and discard risk index (DSRI), donor–recipient BSA ratio; recalculated and capped MELD variants (MELD, MELD-Na, MELD-3); timing from match-run start to centre response and proximity to cross-clamp; and categorical donation type (DBD/DCD), regional groups, and three machine-perfusion definitions (original, FDA-era proxy, and >12-hour cold ischaemia). It also builds the discarded-donor dataset (donors in the PTR in the study period who are not in the liver transplant file), classifies them by donor type and allocation type (AOOS vs sequential).

**aoos_draft6.Rmd** is the R Markdown file that generates the figures and tables for the main manuscript and the supplement.
